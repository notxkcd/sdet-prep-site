Role Fit Analysis

My Script: Role Fit & Aspirations

1. What kind of roles are you looking for?

“I am looking for an SDET or Automation Engineer role where I can apply my 2.5 years of experience in Java/Selenium. I enjoy working in Agile environments where I can be involved from the requirement analysis phase to ensure that we are building testable features from day one.”

2. Why are you looking for a change?

“I’ve had a great experience at Learning Links Foundation, where I helped build their automation foundation. Now, I’m looking to join a more tech-driven product environment where I can tackle larger-scale challenges, perhaps involve myself more in CI/CD pipeline optimization, and work with complex microservices architectures.”

3. Are you comfortable with manual testing?

“Absolutely. I believe a good SDET must first be a great manual tester. You can’t automate what you don’t understand. I always perform exploratory testing on a feature before I even think about writing a script for it.”
