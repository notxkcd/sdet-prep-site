My Script: Behavioral & Agile

1. Tell me about a time you failed.

“In my early days, I once automated a scenario without fully understanding the edge cases. The test passed in my local environment but failed in the nightly build because I hadn’t accounted for a slow-loading third-party API.

What I learned: I realized the importance of using Explicit Waits and robust error handling. Now, I always review my scripts with a ‘failure-first’ mindset to make them more resilient.”

2. How do you handle a conflict with a developer?

“I focus on the data, not the person. If a developer insists a bug isn’t reproducible, I provide them with the exact steps, the test data I used, and the logs from the browser console. If needed, I offer to sit down with them for 5 minutes to show them the issue live. It’s always about the quality of the product, not about being ‘right’.”

3. What is your role in a Sprint Refinement?

“I act as the voice of quality. I ask ‘How are we going to test this?’ and ‘What happens if the user does X?’. I try to find gaps in the requirements before the developers even start coding, which saves us a lot of time later in the sprint.”
