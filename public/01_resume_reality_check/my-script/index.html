My Script: Resume & Experience

1. How I introduce myself (The Summary)

“I am Mohammed Shahid, an Automation Tester with over 2.5 years of experience specializing in Java and Selenium. I spent my tenure at Learning Links Foundation where I played a key role in automating the IntelliLearn EdTech platform. I am proficient in building Hybrid BDD frameworks using Cucumber and TestNG, and I have extensive experience in API testing with Rest Assured and CI/CD integration via Jenkins.”

2. How I explain the 2020-2022 Gap (The Honest Pivot)

“After graduating in 2020, I spent time upskilling in Java and Automation because I wanted to enter the industry with a strong technical foundation rather than just a degree. During this period, I also took on freelance QA assignments and personal projects which eventually led to my role at Learning Links Foundation. I used that time to move from theory to practical implementation, which allowed me to hit the ground running when I joined my first project.”

3. My actual role at Learning Links

“I wasn’t just a script writer. I started with manual testing to understand the business logic of ‘IntelliLearn’ and then transitioned into automation. I was responsible for identifying regression candidates and building the Cucumber step definitions that the rest of the team used.”
