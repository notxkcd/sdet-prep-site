My Script: Learning & Growth

1. What are your areas of improvement? (The “Weakness” Question)

“Currently, I am working on deepening my knowledge of Docker and Containerization. While I’ve used Jenkins for CI, I want to learn how to containerize our test environments to make them more portable and consistent across the team. I’m currently taking an online course to bridge this gap.”

2. How do you keep yourself updated?

“I’m a bit of an autodidact. I follow blogs like ‘Test Automation University’ and I’m part of several QA communities on LinkedIn. Whenever I face a new challenge—like a particularly tricky dynamic element—I dive into the documentation or StackOverflow to find the most efficient solution.”

3. What is the most recent thing you learned?

“Recently, I’ve been focusing on Java Streams. I realized that my old way of iterating through lists of WebElements using for loops could be written much more cleanly and efficiently using Streams and Filters.”
