## 1. What was your biggest achievement at Learning Links?
"My biggest achievement was reducing our manual regression cycle from **3 days down to just 4 hours**. I did this by identifying the high-risk areas of the 'IntelliLearn' platform and automating about 50 core end-to-end flows. This allowed our team to release updates much more frequently and with higher confidence."

## 2. How do you quantify the value of your automation?
"I look at two metrics: **Time Saved** and **Bug Leakage**. By automating the smoke tests, we saved the team roughly 10 hours of manual work every week. More importantly, our automation caught several critical 'Login' and 'Payment' bugs before they ever reached the UAT phase."
